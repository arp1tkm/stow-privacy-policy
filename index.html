Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026
In short STOW is designed to keep your saved content on your device. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or send your stash to our servers.

1. What is STOW?

STOW is a browser extension that lets you quickly save content you find while browsing the web so you can access it later.

You can use STOW to save selected text, links, notes, prompts, images, and other content supported by the extension.

2. Information STOW stores

When you choose to save something using STOW, the extension may store information associated with that item, including:

  • The content you explicitly choose to save.
  • The URL of the page where the content was saved.
  • The title of the webpage.
  • The date and time the item was saved.
  • Your item's completion or done status.

STOW only saves webpage content when you explicitly perform an action to save it.

3. Where your data is stored

STOW uses Chrome's local storage system (chrome.storage.local) to store your stash.

Your saved content remains stored locally in your browser and is not automatically uploaded to a STOW server.

4. Data sharing

STOW does not sell, rent, or share your saved content with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

STOW does not use your saved content for advertising or personalized advertising.

5. Analytics and tracking

STOW does not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers to monitor your browsing activity.

STOW does not maintain a server-side profile of your browsing activity.

6. Permissions

STOW requests browser permissions only when they are necessary to provide its functionality.

  • Storage: Used to save your stash locally in your browser.
  • Clipboard: Used when you explicitly choose to copy saved content.
  • Context menus: Used to provide an option to stash selected content through the browser's right-click menu.
  • Webpage access: Used to detect and save content that you explicitly choose to stash.
  • Tabs: Used to associate saved items with their original webpage URL and title.

7. Children's privacy

STOW is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to STOW or its privacy practices.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or STOW's privacy practices, you can contact the developer through the contact information provided on the STOW Chrome Web Store listing.
